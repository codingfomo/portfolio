01 // EDITOR'S NOTE

DEAR READER,

I have a slightly embarrassing habit of turning everything into a story. A simple conversation at a coffee shop becomes an observation about human behavior. A poorly phrased subway advertisement becomes an impromptu copywriting case study. A random thought at 2 AM becomes a multi-paragraph essay saved in my Notes app.

Somewhere between studying business, reading books, scrolling through the internet, and observing everyday life, I became obsessed with a single, persistent question:

“Why do some words make us stop scrolling — while others disappear into the white noise of the internet?”

I write to figure that out.

Sometimes that means crafting a 1,500-word deep dive into brand strategy. Sometimes it means deleting 20 redundant words from a single headline until the one word that actually matters finally gets room to breathe. And sometimes it means staring at a blank document for an unreasonable amount of time asking: Is this clear? Is this honest? Does anyone actually care?

This little digital magazine is a collection of all of it — the stories I've written, the things I've noticed, the copywriting experiments I've run, and the logic behind how I make boring things interesting.

Welcome to Issue 01.

10 // MY RULES

THE WRITER'S TOOLKIT

01

START LATER.

The interesting part is usually buried three sentences down. Delete your intro.

02

BE SPECIFIC.

“Nice” means nothing. “High quality” is invisible. Describe the exact detail.

03

READ IT OUT LOUD.

If your voice stumbles or you wouldn't say it in real life, rewrite it.

04

KILL YOUR FAVOURITE SENTENCE.

If a line doesn't advance the story, it doesn't get special treatment.

05

CURIOSITY BEATS CLEVERNESS.

Don't try to impress the reader. Give them a reason to continue.
